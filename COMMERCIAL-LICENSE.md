## Why a separate commercial license

OIE-PCS-1.0 grants You a license only for **Personal Non-Commercial
Use** (LICENSE §2). For any **Commercial Use** (LICENSE §1,
definition of "Commercial Use"), You must obtain a separate written

A commercial **code** license does not, by itself, grant rights to
the Brand. Brand rights are licensed separately (see below).


## What is Commercial Use

The full definition is in LICENSE §1. The most common examples are:

- using the Software inside a for-profit organization for any
  business purpose (internal tool, customer delivery, paid support,
  etc.);
- bundling the Software with a commercial hardware product,
  commercial software product, or paid service;
- offering the Software (or a service built on it) as a paid cloud
  service, paid API, or paid managed offering;
- using the Software in advertising, marketing, or other
  revenue-generating activity;
- using the Software in a product whose value is included in the
  price of another product or service (including "free" tiers that
  subsidize paid tiers).

If you are uncertain whether Your use is Commercial Use, treat it
as Commercial Use and contact us, or use a Legacy Community Release
under Apache-2.0 instead.


## What a commercial code license may grant

A commercial code license may, at the Project Copyright Holder's
discretion, grant rights including:

- closed-source integration of the Software into Your product;
- internal deployment within Your organization;
- commercial Distribution of the Software (modified or unmodified);
- providing the Software as part of a paid cloud / SaaS / API /
  managed offering;
- technical support and upgrade rights;
- customization, OEM bundling, and hardware integration;
- indemnification (where commercially reasonable);
- specified scope: number of devices, named users, regions, duration.

Unless the commercial license expressly waives LICENSE §3, the
source-availability obligations of LICENSE §3 for modified Core
Components (CORE-COMPONENTS.md) remain in full force. In practice,
most commercial licenses waive LICENSE §3 for the modified files
inside the customer's own internal deployment, while still requiring
LICENSE §3 disclosure if the customer Distributes modified Core
Components as a competing product.


## What a commercial license does NOT grant (unless separately
agreed)

A commercial code license does NOT, by itself, grant:

- rights to the **Brand** — see TRADEMARKS.md and the commercial
  Brand license section below;
- rights to **third-party components** included in or used by the
  Software — see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES;
- rights to **patents** held by the Project Copyright Holder beyond
  what is expressly granted — see LICENSE §7;
- rights to **resell or sublicense** the commercial license to a
  third party, unless expressly permitted;
- any **warranty**, beyond what is expressly stated in the
  commercial agreement.


## How to obtain a commercial code license

To request a commercial code license, please contact the Project
Copyright Holder with the following information:

1. Your organization name and primary contact (name, role, email,
   phone).
2. Brief description of Your product or service.
3. How You plan to integrate, distribute, or operate the Software.
4. Estimated deployment size (number of devices, users, regions,
   requests per month, etc.).
5. Preferred commercial license term (perpetual, subscription,
   one-off, etc.).
6. Any specific needs: indemnification, support tier, custom SLA,
   OEM bundling, etc.

**Contact email**: please open a GitHub issue at
https://github.com/63894696/oi_enhancements/issues with the
"commercial-license" label, or follow the contact link on the
project's GitHub README.

**Expected response time**: 5–10 business days for an initial
discussion. Actual license execution typically takes 2–4 weeks
depending on scope, indemnification needs, and counterparty review.

## Commercial Brand license

If, in addition to Commercial Use of the code, You also want to use
the **Brand** (the names, logos, icons — see TRADEMARKS.md) for
Your Commercial product or service, You need a separate **commercial
Brand license**.

Common Brand-license scenarios:

- You Distribute a forked/derivative work under Your own name and
  icon. → You do NOT need a Brand license; You are not using the
  upstream Brand.
- You Distribute Your product as "Powered by Prisir AI" or similar
  co-branding. → You MAY do this for free under TRADEMARKS.md
  Permitted Uses (factual reference), but You MUST clearly indicate
  Your product is not the official OI Enhancements project.
- You want to use the actual Prisir AI logo on Your commercial
  packaging. → You need a Brand license; contact us.
- You want to use "Prisir AI" as the name of Your own competing
  product. → Refused; You must choose a different name.

For Brand license inquiries, use the same contact channel as
commercial code licenses.
